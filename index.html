About

Robotics has always been a mix of curiosity and problem solving for me — figuring out how a machine can make sense of the world, move through it, and keep doing the right thing when reality gets in the way.

That curiosity started with mechatronics and pulled me toward the software side of robotics, where sensing, perception, estimation, and control come together. I've worked with cameras, LiDAR, GPS, IMUs, and different robotic platforms, learning that the most interesting challenges often emerge where these systems interact.

At Northeastern University's Field Robotics Lab, I've built an asynchronous stereo camera calibration system, developed perception and localization solutions across multiple sensors, and deployed robotics software on platforms including Unitree and Boston Dynamics Spot. Beyond research, I've built systems involving SLAM, 3D reconstruction, motion planning, and reinforcement learning.

Different problems, same approach: build something, see where it breaks, understand why, and make it better.

Outside of robotics, you'll find me exploring new music, stargazing, searching for satellites in the night sky, or thinking about my dream car.
